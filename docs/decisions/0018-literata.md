# 0018. Literata replaces Publico Text (amends 0015)

**Decided.** The serif (model replies, headings, accents) is *Literata*, bundled in `App/Fonts` as its two variable files (roman and italic, weight and optical-size axes) under the SIL Open Font License. Publico Text and the Iowan Old Style fallback are gone. DM Sans and iA Writer Mono stay as decided in [0015](0015-typography.md).

**Why.** The author preferred it after trying it. Literata was drawn for long reading on screens, it carries the warmth the brief asks for, and being bundled it looks the same on every Mac from the first launch, with no download or fallback face.

**Cost.** About 1.9 MB more in the app bundle.
