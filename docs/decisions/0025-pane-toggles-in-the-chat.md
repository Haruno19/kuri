# 0025. The panes' toggles are drawn by the chat, with system symbols

**Decided.** Both panes' toggles are glass circles drawn over the chat's top corners: the sidebar's just past the traffic lights (or at the chat's leading edge while the sidebar is open), the inspector's top right. Neither is a toolbar item, so both stay in place in full screen, where the toolbar only slides in with the menu bar. Their icons are the system's `sidebar.left` and `sidebar.right`, as in Xcode, instead of pencil-drawn ones. This amends [0021](0021-inspector.md), where the sidebar's toggle was a toolbar item of the chat's column.

**Why.** In full screen macOS draws the toolbar in a window of its own and backs it with an opaque band, whatever the toolbar's background: it covered the tops of the chat and the inspector, and the sidebar's toggle, as a toolbar item, disappeared with the toolbar until the pointer reached the top of the screen, while the inspector's stayed. Drawn by the chat, both behave the same in a window and in full screen. Toolbar items with the system's symbols were tried too; they need the toolbar shown in full screen, and with it the band. The symbols are what people know these buttons by in every Mac app with side panes, so those stay.

**Cost.** The sidebar's toggle reads the traffic lights' position from AppKit (the window's zoom button), and an empty, click-through toolbar item keeps the unified title bar's height, without which macOS drops the toolbar and the traffic lights rise out of line with the toggles. The toggles' icons are SF Symbols in a window whose other icons are pencil-drawn.
